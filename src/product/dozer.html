The governance layer for the vibe coding era

Dozer sits on your side of the table. It is an agent-neutral workspace that organizes your agents, shares their context, audits every execution, checks what they built, and hands back a result you can accept — or send back.

An open, composable workspace

A thin, stable Host. A validated reference suite. Room for anyone to replace or extend every step.

Micro Host

STATUS: IN_DEV

The Host owns windows, layout, permissions, plugin lifecycle, and the workflow kernel — and nothing domain-specific. It stays small so it can stay stable.

PLT_01

Out-of-Process Plugins

STATUS: PLANNING

Plugins run as separate processes over a versioned protocol. Rebuild and restart a plugin without relinking Dozer, and a crash takes nothing else down.

PLT_02

STATUS: PLANNING
